The problem

Most scheduling tools ask the organizer to guess time slots up front — then everyone compares answers by hand.

  • event_busy
    Fixed slots miss real availability

    When you pre-pick times, respondents can only say yes or no to your guesses — not when they can actually meet.

  • table
    Spreadsheets don't rank tradeoffs

    A grid shows overlap, but not which time balances attendance, preferences, and must-have attendees.

  • group
    Group scheduling shouldn't need accounts

    People just want a link to respond — not another login before they can say when they're free.

Why ScheduleSnap

ScheduleSnap flips the usual flow: you set broad scheduling windows, respondents mark their own availability inside them, and recommendations appear automatically.

Rank preferences from 1–5, flag critical attendees, and pick from balanced suggestions instead of manually scanning every response.
