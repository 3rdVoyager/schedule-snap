About ScheduleSnap

A free, link-based scheduling tool that finds the best time for your group to meet — without accounts or spreadsheet gymnastics.

Why it exists

Most scheduling tools ask the organizer to guess time slots up front. ScheduleSnap flips that: you set scheduling windows, respondents mark their own availability, and ranked recommendations help you pick a time that balances who can attend, how much they like each option, and who absolutely has to be there.

The goal is simple — make group scheduling fast enough that people actually finish it instead of leaving it in a chat thread.

Open source

ScheduleSnap is open source under the MIT License. The frontend is static HTML and JavaScript; the API runs on Cloudflare Workers with D1. You can inspect the code, report issues, or contribute on GitHub.
