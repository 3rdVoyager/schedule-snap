Use Cases

One link-based flow for groups that need a real answer — not another round of “when works for everyone?”

Who it’s for

ScheduleSnap works when people have different schedules but share one event to plan.

  • Teams & projects

    Standups, sprint planning, and cross-team syncs — set a window, collect availability, and pick a time that balances attendance and preferences.

  • Classes & study groups

    Office hours, review sessions, and group projects. Students mark when they can meet without signing up for an account.

  • Clubs & communities

    Recurring meetups, volunteer shifts, and event planning. Flag critical organizers so recommendations always include key people.

  • Friends & family

    Dinners, trips, and game nights. Share one link, rank how much you like each option, and stop scrolling through group chat polls.

Why it fits these groups

  • link
    No accounts to respond

    Participants open a link, add availability, and done — ideal for one-off events and casual groups.

  • schedule
    Windows instead of fixed slots

    You define when the event could happen; everyone marks their own times inside those bounds.

  • recommend
    Ranked recommendations

    See which times maximize attendance, honor preferences, and include must-have attendees — without manual spreadsheet work.
